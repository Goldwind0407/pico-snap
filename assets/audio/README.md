These short effects are original procedural recordings generated by
`tool/generate_sounds.py` using sine oscillators, filtered noise and envelopes.
No samples or recordings from another product are included. Run the script
with Python 3 to reproduce the mono, 22.05 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV files.

tick / ready: soft countdown tones
shutter: a two-part mechanical shutter
feed: a short instant-print motor and paper feed
peel / stamp: paper adhesive and a soft contact thump
complete: a brief ascending three-note confirmation
pop: choosing something to make
select: a light tap for picking an option
snip: two scissor closes, a finished cut-out
sparkle: scattered bells, a shiny finish or template
undo / redo: two notes stepping down / up
trash: paper crumpling, after a confirmed delete
whoosh / flip: filtered-noise air, sharing / turning a page
oops: two soft low notes when something didn't work
