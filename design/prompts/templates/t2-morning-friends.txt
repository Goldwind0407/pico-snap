A flat graphic design of a vertical photo booth strip template in the Korean "four cuts" style, front view, perfectly straight, filling the whole image edge to edge. Aspect ratio 1:3 (tall and narrow). If the image has to be wider than 1:3, place the strip in the center on a plain pure white background.
No people, no photos, no hands, no table, no shadow, no perspective, no mockup - only the flat printable template.

Layout (very important):
- Four identical empty photo windows stacked vertically, each a perfect sharp-cornered rectangle with a 4:3 landscape ratio.
- All four windows have exactly the same size, are centered horizontally, have about 6% side margins and small equal gaps between them.
- The bottom 10% of the strip, below the last window, is a footer area.
- Fill every photo window with solid flat pure green #00FF00: no gradient, no texture, no pattern, nothing drawn inside the green.
- Never use bright green anywhere else in the design.
- Decorations live on the frame around the windows. They may overlap a window corner slightly, but never cover more than a small corner of any window.

Style: cute, polished, print quality, clean vector-like illustration, soft pastel colors, consistent line weight, legible lettering. Only the text written below, no other words.

Theme:
Warm cream and blush pink frame with scattered hand-drawn confetti squiggles and dots. Cute hand-drawn food characters with simple smiling faces - a fried egg, a sweet potato, a red apple - peeking over the window edges. A small white speech bubble near the top saying "say cheese~". Footer: a sweet potato character waving, with the handwritten text "have a nice day".
